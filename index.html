Welcome to The Red Lion!

You are in a traditional British pub restaurant in London. It's Friday evening and you are hungry. The waiter is going to help you – but you have to speak English!

Du bist in einem typisch britischen Pub-Restaurant in London. Bestelle auf Englisch Getränke und Essen, reagiere auf die Fragen des Kellners, löse ein kleines Problem und bezahle die Rechnung.

Your tasks

  1. Ask for a table.
  2. Order a drink, a starter, a main course and a dessert.
  3. Answer the waiter's questions (sides, allergies …).
  4. Complain politely if something goes wrong.
  5. Ask for the bill and pay.

Tip: Be polite! Use please, thank you, I'd like …, Could I have …?
